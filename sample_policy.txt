REFERENCE POLICY — SAMPLE FOR TESTING

1. Payment Terms
All invoices must be paid within 30 days of the invoice date.

2. Confidentiality
Confidential information must remain protected during the agreement and for 3 years after termination.

3. Liability
The contract must clearly specify the maximum aggregate liability.

4. Intellectual Property
Ownership of intellectual property created under the agreement must be explicitly documented.

5. Dispute Resolution
The agreement must specify a formal dispute-resolution process.
