# SOFTWARE SERVICE CONTRACT REVIEW POLICY

## 1. Payment
Payment terms must clearly state the amount, billing frequency, and payment deadline.

## 2. Service Availability
The agreement should define a measurable service availability target, such as 99.9% monthly uptime.

## 3. Data Protection
The service provider must protect customer data and use it only for authorized purposes.

## 4. Confidentiality
Confidentiality obligations must apply to both parties and cover appropriate handling of confidential information.

## 5. Termination
Termination provisions must specify notice requirements and any applicable exceptions.

## 6. Liability
The agreement should define an appropriate liability cap and explain relevant exclusions.

## 7. Security Incidents
The agreement should specify a notification deadline for security incidents, such as notification within 72 hours of discovery.

## 8. Governing Law
The agreement must identify the governing law and jurisdiction.

**Review note:** These are fictional testing requirements, not legal advice. A reviewer must assess applicability and context before making any conclusion.
